Build with CI.

People. Ideas. A collective.

Already redesigning it in your head? Good.

Be part of CI

Some people see something that could be better and move on.

Others start thinking about what they could do differently.

We like those people.

Build with CI brings together clinicians, designers, engineers, filmmakers, researchers, educators, founders and people who don’t fit neatly into a category.

Different backgrounds. Different skills. A shared instinct to make things happen.

Find your people.

The people who challenge your thinking. Back your ideas. Bring skills you don’t have. See possibilities you might have missed.

People you can learn from, create with and call when you’ve got something you want to build.

Sometimes it’s a conversation. Sometimes it’s helping someone with a small project. Sometimes it’s the beginning of something much bigger.

Not another network of names.

People who actually build together.

Be part of CI.

We’re bringing together a small founding collective of curious, capable people who want to make things better.

People who care about their craft. Who share what they know. Who are willing to learn from others. Who want to contribute to something beyond their own work.

You don’t need to have an idea ready.

You don’t need to work in healthcare.

You just need to bring something of yourself.

Help shape what CI becomes.

Be part of CI

Got something you want to build?

An idea you’ve been sitting on. A project that’s missing the right people. A problem you think deserves a better answer.

Bring it to CI.

We’ll explore what you’re trying to create, where you need help and whether we could build something worthwhile together.

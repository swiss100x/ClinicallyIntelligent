# Clinically Intelligent: standing site rules

Keep the personality. Improve the architecture.

## Two worlds
- Editorial (near-black #0F0E0C, ivory #EFE9DC, amber #E9A35A): conviction, the argument, the publication.
- Human (warm cream #F4EFE4, paper #FBF8F1, ink #0C0B0A, forest green #1F5F3F): routes, the founder, invitations.
- Problems (/problems/): forest green on the site. It is simply "Problems"; never call it a "bank". Amber #D98A1E for the one main action on a screen.

## Type
- Fraunces for headings and editorial lines. Inter Tight for body, interface and buttons.
- Times New Roman / Tinos only for the founder's own writing ("I am Juwon" and /founder/).

## Composition
- The frame is shared: logo, colours, type, voice. The composition is not.
- Each page behaves like its own object: the publication is a library, Problems (/problems/) is a card you fill in and post, Build with CI is a poster.
- Not every page is a promotion. Problems stays minimal: logo only, and only two onward options after submitting (the newsletter and Build with us).
- Warm and human first: real paper, real ticks, real handwriting space. No decoration for its own sake.
- Immersion is the signature: the person should feel they are doing the thing (filling in the card, taking an issue off the shelf), not reading about it. Centre the object, give it presence, let it respond.

## Voice
- First person where it's Juwon. Specific moments over general claims ("the note you still haven't finished at 4:42pm").
- Keep "One tool. One real clinical use. One way to apply it this week." and "AI does not fix the NHS."
- No em or en dashes. No arrows in button text. No uppercase eyebrow labels. No middle-dot lists.

## Integrity
- Label work as published, delivered, concept or developing. Never invent clients, results, patients or an NHS endorsement.
- CI is independent of Juwon's NHS employment. Say "consumer AI tools" when warning about patient information.

## Assets
- Website logo: assets/ci-logo.png only. The founder photo appears on /founder/ only.
- Real CI work only: issue covers, Field Guides, film frames. No stock clinicians, robots or glowing brains.

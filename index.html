I am Juwon.

NHS occupational therapist, exploring AI in clinical practice. East London.

I started Clinically Intelligent during time away from the ward. I was using AI tools in my own work and they were making a difference. Not in a theoretical way. In an I finished my notes before the end of shift way. In an I found a guideline update my Trust had not circulated way. In an I had more mental space left at the end of a Tuesday way.

Every tool I cover I have tried myself first. Every scenario comes from real clinical work. If it did not earn its place, it does not make the newsletter.

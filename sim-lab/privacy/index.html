How we look after your feedback

CI Sim Lab is a small private trial. Here’s the simple version of what happens to what you tell us.

What we collect

Your answers to five multiple choice questions, and your comment if you choose to write one. With them we keep which experience and version you tried, whether you used “Report a problem” or the end of the experience, the time you sent it, and a random code made by your browser for this visit. The code lets us tell one visit from another. It isn’t linked to you.

No name, no email and no account. We don’t record what you choose inside the experience, and we don’t use tracking or advertising cookies.

Who reads it

Juwon, who runs Clinically Intelligent. Nobody else. Feedback is held by our form provider, Basin, on our behalf.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months, then it’s deleted.

What we do with it

We use it to improve the experience and decide what to build next. We may share overall themes, always anonymised. Your individual answers and comments are never published.

Please leave out

Anything that could identify a patient, a colleague or your workplace.

Not for incidents or support

This is a learning experience, not clinical decision support and not an NHS reporting system. For a patient safety incident or concern, use your organisation’s usual process. If the experience brought something up for you, talk to someone you trust or your usual wellbeing support.

Questions, or want something removed?

Email hello@clinicallyintelligent.com. If you tell us roughly when you sent it, we’ll find it and remove it.

How we look after your card

It’s a suggestion box. No name needed. Here’s the simple version of what happens to what you write.

Who reads it

Juwon, who runs Clinically Intelligent. Nobody else.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months, then it’s deleted.

What we do with it

We look for patterns across cards, the things that come up again and again, and use them to decide what to write about, teach and build. We may turn those patterns into themes or research, always anonymised. Your card itself is never published.

Your email

Only if you choose to add it, and only so we can follow up about your card. We won’t add you to any mailing list.

Please leave out

Anything that could identify a patient or a colleague. Keep it about the work.

Not for incidents

This isn’t an NHS reporting system. For a patient safety incident or concern, use your organisation’s usual process.

Questions, or want something removed?

Email hello@clinicallyintelligent.com and we’ll sort it.

Clinically Intelligent is independent and not affiliated with Barts Health NHS Trust.

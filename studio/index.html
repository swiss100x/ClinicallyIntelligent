Clinically Intelligent is a clinician-led initiative exploring better ways to learn and work in healthcare.

We combine practical education, clinical insight and thoughtful design, starting with AI and exploring how new capabilities translate into meaningful change. Founded by Juwon Akinyande, an NHS occupational therapist in East London.

Learning through experience

Would you send it?

A clinician's note, and an AI summary of it. Tap the words the AI added that aren't in the source.

The source note

Lantus 20 units normally. Reduce to 18 units on football days.

The AI summary

Where did “before football” come from? Nowhere.

The numbers were right. The meaning wasn’t.

Every figure survived. The instruction didn’t. “On football days” and “before football” are different advice, and a patient would act on the second. This is the habit CI teaches: check every AI draft for what was added, what was removed, whether the meaning shifted, and whether it now sounds more certain than the source.

This is how we think learning should feel: practical, active and built around judgement.

How we approach the work

Start with the work. Develop capability. Measure what changes.

  1. Understand the workIdentify real healthcare challenges and opportunities for improvement.
  2. Learn through experienceDesign engaging learning experiences that support exploration, practice and feedback.
  3. Protect professional judgementEvaluate technology and use it safely, critically and responsibly.
  4. Apply learningConnect new capabilities to appropriate workplace activities.
  5. Measure and improveAssess competence, examine what changes and refine the approach.

Learning is the start.
Change is the test.

Understand problems. Develop capability. Measure what changes.

We’re interested in what happens beyond learning: what gets applied, what changes in practice, what gets in the way and what can be improved. These are questions we’re setting out to investigate, not results we’re claiming.

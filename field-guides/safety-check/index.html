The Clinician's AI Safety Check.

A 30 second, 9 question framework for NHS clinicians using consumer AI tools at work.

Free · 30 seconds · 9 questions · 3 stages
Get the Field Guide → See what's inside ↓

Eight pages. Three stages. Nine questions. One check to run every time, before proceeding, or not.

01 / WHAT'S INSIDE

A practical field guide for clinicians using AI under real time pressure.

i.

The nine-question framework to run before, during, and after every AI session at work.

ii.

What clinicians should never paste into consumer AI tools, and how to recognise identifying combinations that single details miss.

iii.

A side-by-side composite scenario worked example. Safe inputs. Unsafe inputs. The difference matters.

iv.

How to think about AI governance without getting overwhelmed. Plain English, no policy soup.

v.

A clear five-step process for if something goes wrong. Reported mistakes get managed. Hidden ones become incidents.

vi.

The questions your IG lead can answer, and how to find them before you need them.

02 / WHY THIS EXISTS

AI entered clinical practice before governance caught up.

Clinicians are already using these tools. The unease is already there. What's missing is a way to think clearly about safety in the thirty seconds between opening the tool and pasting the first line.

Trust policies are catching up slowly. Most clinicians do not know who their IG lead is until they urgently need them. The gap between the technology and the governance is currently sitting on the clinician's shoulders, alone.

This Field Guide is the smallest useful thing that closes that gap.

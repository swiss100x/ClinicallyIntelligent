01 / WHAT'S INSIDE

A structured approach to writing prompts that produce reliable AI outputs.

R
Role
Set the tone, expertise, and lens the AI takes before it says a word.
T
Task
Name what you want done. Precise, single-purpose, unambiguous.
C
Context
Give the AI the information it needs to make good decisions.
O
Output
Define the shape, length, and format of what comes back.
R
Reasoning
Ask the AI to think before it answers. Structured thinking produces structured outputs.
C
Constraint
Set the boundaries. What must be included, avoided, or true.

03 / WHY THIS EXISTS

The problem is rarely the tool. It is almost always the instructions.

Most clinicians have tried AI and found it unreliable. They ask vague questions. They rewrite the same prompt repeatedly. They blame the model. Usually, the model is capable. The instructions are just incomplete.

When these prompts fail, clinicians conclude that AI is not ready for clinical work. It becomes the tool they tried once and never returned to. The gap between what AI can do and what it does for you sits almost entirely in the prompt.

This guide teaches the structure. Six parts. Same structure every time.

04 / WHY THIS EXISTS

The problem is rarely the tool. It is almost always the instructions.

Many healthcare professionals have tried AI and found it unreliable. They ask vague questions. They rewrite the same prompt repeatedly. They blame the model. Usually, the model is capable. The instructions are just incomplete.

When prompts fail, professionals conclude that AI is not ready for clinical work. It becomes the tool they tried once and never returned to. The gap between what AI can do and what it does for you sits almost entirely in the prompt.

Don't memorise prompts. Memorise the pattern.

ROLE → CONTEXT → TASK → OUTPUT → CONSTRAINTS → SOURCES → IF UNKNOWN
